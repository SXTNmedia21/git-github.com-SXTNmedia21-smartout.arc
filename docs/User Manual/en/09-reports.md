# Reports and Reconciliation

> Daily reconciliation, KPI dashboard, seasonal review and report types — data-driven insights for better operations.

---

## Overview

SmartOut's reporting module gives you insight into operations through three levels: daily reconciliation, ongoing KPI tracking and season-based evaluation. Everything builds on data collected automatically through the shift schedule, operational sessions and time clock.

---

## Daily reconciliation

Daily reconciliation is the process where the day's operations are summarised and approved:

1. **Automatic summary** — The system collects data from all operational sessions
2. **Cash reconciliation** — Register cash and card turnover (OCR support for POS receipts)
3. **Deviation report** — Overview of incomplete tasks, temperature deviations and absences
4. **Manager approval** — The manager signs the reconciliation digitally

| Data            | Source                 |
| --------------- | ---------------------- |
| Working hours   | Time clock             |
| Task completion | Operational sessions   |
| Revenue         | Cash reconciliation    |
| Temperature log | HACCP routines         |
| Deviations      | Automatic registration |

> The OCR function lets you take a photo of the POS receipt with your phone. SmartOut reads the numbers automatically.

---

## KPI dashboard

The KPI dashboard gives a real-time overview of key figures:

### Operational KPIs

- **Task completion** — Percentage of assigned tasks that are completed
- **Readiness score** — Average onboarding progression
- **HACCP compliance** — Proportion of approved temperature logs
- **Absence** — Absence percentage per department

### Financial KPIs

- **Labour cost per revenue** — Staff cost as a share of income
- **Revenue per working hour** — Productivity measurement
- **Budget vs. actual** — Deviation from planned staffing
- **Overtime cost** — Total overtime hours and costs

### Personnel KPIs

- **Turnover** — Staff turnover per period
- **Training time** — Average time from trainee to active
- **Competence coverage** — Proportion of employees with the right certifications

---

## Seasonal review

At the end of a season, SmartOut generates a complete season report:

- **Totals** — Total revenue, working hours, labour cost
- **Trends** — Development over the season with graphs
- **Comparison** — Against previous season or budget
- **Top and bottom** — Best and weakest departments/periods
- **Recommendations** — AI-generated suggestions for improvements

---

## Report types

| Report               | Frequency      | Recipient                    |
| -------------------- | -------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Daily reconciliation | Daily          | Manager, admin               |
| Weekly report        | Weekly         | Admin, owner                 |
| Payroll report       | Per pay period | Admin                        |
| HACCP report         | On request     | Admin, Food Safety Authority |
| Season report        | Per season     | Owner                        |
| Competence report    | On request     | Admin                        |

> All reports can be exported as PDF or CSV.

---

## Automatic alerts

SmartOut sends automatic alerts based on KPI thresholds:

- **Absence above limit** — Alerts when the absence percentage exceeds the configured level
- **Budget deviation** — Alerts when labour cost deviates more than 10% from budget
- **HACCP breach** — Immediate alert on temperature deviation
- **Low readiness** — Alerts when new employees are not making progress
