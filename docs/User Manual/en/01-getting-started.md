# Getting Started

> Create an account, configure your company, set up a workspace, and invite employees — everything you need to start with SmartOut.

---

## Create an account

Go to smartout.ai and click "Get started". You can register with email and password, or sign in with Google or Microsoft SSO.

1. **Visit the registration page** — Click "Start free trial" on smartout.ai.
2. **Fill in your information** — Enter your full name, email address and choose a secure password (at least 8 characters).
3. **Confirm your email** — You will receive a confirmation email. Click the link to activate your account.

> Alternatively, you can sign in with Google or Microsoft. This means you don't need to create a password.

After registration, your user identity is created automatically. You then manage everything from the dashboard.

---

## Register your company

After signing in, the company registration begins. SmartOut uses this to tailor the platform for your industry.

1. **Company name** — The legal name of the company.
2. **Organisation number** — SmartOut uses the Bronnoysund Register Centre to fetch public data automatically.
3. **Industry** — Choose between restaurant, hotel, cafe, bar, catering or other.
4. **Country and currency** — Norway (NOK) is the default. Sweden, Denmark and Finland are also supported.

> SmartOut automatically retrieves public information from the Bronnoysund Register Centre based on the organisation number. This includes the registered address, company type and industry.

### AI-assisted setup

After the company is registered, SmartOut uses AI tools to analyse your business. The platform:

- Searches for websites, reviews and menu information
- Suggests departments, locations and position types based on what it finds
- Gives you a ready-made recommended structure that you can approve or customise

---

## Set up a workspace

A **workspace** is the operational unit in SmartOut — typically a single outlet, a restaurant, or a hotel department. All daily operations happen within a workspace.

### Configure the structure

| Element    | What it means                      | Example                         |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Department | What — the professional unit       | Kitchen, Floor, Bar             |
| Location   | Where — the physical placement     | Main building, Terrace, Storage |
| Team       | Who — the access and working group | Evening crew, Brunch team       |
| Position   | What function per shift            | Head waiter, Chef, Bartender    |

> Departments are permanent. Teams can be seasonal. Positions are assigned per shift, not per person.

### Opening hours and schedule

Configure weekly opening hours for the workspace and departments. This forms the basis for automatic generation of operational sessions and shift schedules.

---

## Invite employees

Once the structure is in place, you can invite employees. SmartOut supports several methods:

1. **Email invitation** — Enter the email address. The employee receives an invitation link.
2. **SMS invitation** — Send the invitation via SMS for faster response.
3. **QR code** — Generate a QR code that employees can scan with their phone.
4. **Manual creation** — Create user accounts directly and share login details.

Each invited user starts as a **trainee**. In trainee mode, they complete training modules, sign documents and fulfil all required protocols — without affecting live data.

---

## Next steps

Once the company, workspace and employees are in place, you can:

- Configure the **shift schedule** to start planning shifts
- Set up **protocols** and **policies** for training
- Activate **communication** for team chat and notifications
- Let the **Lise AI assistant** help you get started faster

SmartOut is designed to be ready to use within minutes — not days.
