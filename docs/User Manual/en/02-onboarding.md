# Onboarding

> Trainee mode, module journeys, protocol training and readiness score — this is how SmartOut gets new employees ready for work.

---

## What is onboarding in SmartOut?

Onboarding is the process that takes a new hire from "invited" to "ready for work". SmartOut defines "ready" as: all assigned policies are learned, all protocols are completed — knowledge tested, procedures trained, confirmations signed.

> An employee who has completed all onboarding has a **readiness score** of 100%. Trainee mode ensures this happens before the employee works with live data.

---

## Trainee mode

All new employees start in trainee mode. This is a sandbox environment where they:

- See the real user interface, but without affecting live data
- Complete training modules at their own pace
- Are tested on knowledge through quizzes
- Sign necessary documents (employment contract, HACCP confirmations, health and safety declarations)

### Progress tracking

The manager can follow progress from the dashboard:

| Status indicator  | Meaning                                          |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Not started       | Trainee has not opened the module yet            |
| In progress       | Trainee has started but not completed            |
| Awaiting approval | Trainee has completed, but manager must sign off |
| Completed         | Module is approved                               |

> If a trainee does not make progress within 48 hours, the system automatically escalates to the nearest manager.

---

## Module journeys

A module journey is a sequence of training steps tailored to the employee's role and department. SmartOut generates the module journey automatically based on:

- **Department** — Kitchen staff get HACCP modules, floor staff get service modules
- **Role** — Managers get additional modules on personnel responsibility and deviation reporting
- **Season** — Seasonal modules are only activated during relevant periods

### Content in a module journey

Each module can contain:

- **Procedure** — Step-by-step instructions (e.g. "How to open the restaurant")
- **Knowledge test** — Multiple-choice quiz to verify understanding
- **Confirmation** — Digital signature to confirm that the content is understood
- **Routine** — Tasks to be performed regularly after training

---

## Readiness score

Readiness score is a percentage that shows how ready an employee is for independent work.

**Calculation:** Number of completed protocols divided by total number of assigned protocols.

- **0-49%** — Trainee. Not ready for independent work.
- **50-79%** — Partially ready. Can work under supervision.
- **80-99%** — Almost ready. Missing a few modules.
- **100%** — Fully ready. Can work independently.

> Readiness score is visible to the manager in real time. The employee sees their own progress in the "My Training" view.

---

## After onboarding

When readiness score reaches 100%, the profile switches from **trainee** to **active**. The employee:

- Gets access to live data and operational tasks
- Can take their own shifts and perform operational tasks
- Appears in the shift schedule as an available resource
- Retains access to training materials for reference
