# Staff Management

> Organisation structure, profiles, roles, competence tracking — manage your team effectively.

---

## Organisation structure

SmartOut organises employees through a hierarchical structure with four building blocks:

| Element        | Description                                 | Permanent?                |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| **Department** | Professional unit (Kitchen, Floor, Bar)     | Yes                       |
| **Location**   | Physical placement (Main building, Terrace) | Yes                       |
| **Team**       | Working group (Evening crew, Brunch team)   | Can be seasonal           |
| **Position**   | Function per shift (Chef, Head Waiter)      | Per shift, not per person |

> Departments are permanent and rarely change. Teams can be created and dissolved by season. Positions are assigned per shift — a person can have different positions on different shifts.

---

## Profiles

Each employee has a **profile** linked to a specific workspace. A person can have profiles in multiple workspaces.

### Profile information

- **Display name** — The name shown in the dashboard
- **Role** — employee, manager, admin or owner
- **Status** — trainee, active, inactive or offboarding
- **Department** — Primary department
- **Competences** — Completed protocols and certifications
- **Contact info** — Email, phone, emergency contact

### Profile status

| Status          | Description                                                 |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Trainee**     | New hire in training. Sandbox mode — no impact on live data |
| **Active**      | Fully operational employee with access to all features      |
| **Inactive**    | On leave or pause. Not shown in shift schedule              |
| **Offboarding** | In the process of leaving. Limited access                   |

---

## Roles and access

SmartOut has four role levels with increasing access:

1. **Employee** — Can view their own shift schedule, complete tasks, chat
2. **Manager** — Everything in employee + can edit shift schedule, approve absences, view reports for their team
3. **Admin** — Everything in manager + can configure workspace, invite employees, manage protocols
4. **Owner** — Everything in admin + can manage company, subscription and billing

> Roles apply per workspace. A person can be admin in one workspace and employee in another.

### Team leader

Team leader is not a role, but a property on the team. The team leader has extended visibility into their own team, but no extra system access beyond their normal role.

## Competence tracking

SmartOut tracks each employee's competence through completed protocols:

- **Protocols** — Which policies the employee has completed
- **Certifications** — HACCP courses, allergen handling, fire safety, etc.
- **Expiry dates** — Alerts when certifications are approaching renewal
- **Readiness score** — Percentage completion of all assigned requirements

> Competence data is automatically used in shift planning — the system alerts if you try to assign a shift to someone without the right competence.

## Employee views

### For managers

- **Staff list** — Overview of all employees with filtering by department, status and role
- **Competence matrix** — Visual overview of who can do what
- **Progress report** — Track onboarding and training status

### For employees

- **My profile** — View and update your own contact information
- **My training** — Overview of assigned and completed modules
- **My CV** — Exportable overview of competences and experience
