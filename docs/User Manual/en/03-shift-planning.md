# Shift Planning

> Three view modes, shift cards, templates, availability, shift swaps and time clock — everything you need for shift scheduling.

---

## Overview

The shift schedule is the core of daily operations. Here you plan shifts, assign staff, manage availability and track actual working hours. SmartOut offers three different view modes to give you a full overview.

---

## View modes

### Employee view (person)

Shows the shift schedule organised by employees. Each row is a person, and the columns are days. You can quickly see who is working when, and drag-and-drop shifts between employees.

### Job view (position)

Organised by position types (Chef, Head Waiter, Bartender). Each row is a position, and you can see if there are enough people in each role per day. Useful for ensuring correct staffing.

### Team view

Groups shifts by team. Ideal for seasonal teams or cross-functional groups.

> You can switch between view modes at any time — the data is the same, only the perspective changes.

---

## Shift cards

Each shift is represented as a **shift card** with the following information:

| Field    | Description                                |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Person   | Who is assigned to the shift               |
| Position | What function (Chef, Waiter, etc.)         |
| Time     | Start and end time                         |
| Location | Where the shift takes place                |
| Status   | Planned, confirmed, in progress, completed |
| Tasks    | Associated operational tasks for the shift |

### Shift card details

Click on a shift card to see expanded information:

- **Details** — Time, break, position and notes
- **Functions** — Special tasks for this shift
- **History** — Changes and swaps
- **Pay** — Calculated cost including supplements
- **Tasks** — Assigned operational tasks and procedures

---

## Templates

SmartOut supports shift schedule templates to save time:

1. **Create template from existing week** — Copy a completed shift schedule as a template
2. **Apply template** — Use a saved template on a new week
3. **Automatic generation** — Let SmartOut suggest a shift schedule based on history, budget and availability

---

## Availability

Employees can register their availability directly in the app:

- **Available** — Can work
- **Preferred off** — Would like time off, but can be contacted
- **Unavailable** — Cannot work (holiday, sick leave, other)

> Availability information is displayed directly in the shift schedule with colour codes, so the planner can see conflicts immediately.

---

## Shift swaps

Employees can request to swap shifts with each other:

1. The employee selects the shift they want to swap away
2. The system shows who is available and qualified
3. The other employee accepts the swap request
4. The manager approves (or declines) the swap

> Shift swaps are only possible between employees who have the right competence for the position.

---

## Time clock

SmartOut has an integrated time clock that records actual working hours:

- **Clock in** — Register attendance at shift start
- **Clock out** — Register departure at shift end
- **Break recording** — Document breaks automatically
- **Deviation reporting** — The system alerts about late arrivals or early departures

Time clock data is used for payroll calculation, reporting and KPI tracking.
