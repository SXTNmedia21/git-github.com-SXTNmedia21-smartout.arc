# Sixten — Stage Engine Integration

Sixten is the first heartbeat-coupled agent in Smartout, sitting at the seam between structure and fire — drafting ADRs, briefing councils, setting plan temperature, and orchestrating the build agents that ship code. He wakes when the heartbeat-dispatcher plucks an `engine_state` row whose `mission.persona = 'sixten'` under `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, fires `pg_notify('mission_dispatch', ...)`, and a mission-pool slot loads the `docs/journeys/<slug>/` folder so MISSION.md, LICENSE.md, FLOW.md, and RESCUE-PROMPT.md become the boot-context. Over time he will keep Smartout's build coherent across years — every plan, ADR, mission folder, and council brief carries a falsifiable acceptance test, a why-line, and an intro that points the next reader down the right thread.
