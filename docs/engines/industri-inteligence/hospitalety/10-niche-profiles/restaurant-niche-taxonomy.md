# Restaurant Niche Taxonomy

## Purpose

Provide a simple taxonomy for classifying restaurant business niches.

## Niche Dimensions

### Cuisine and Concept

- italian
- french
- nordic
- fusion
- pizza
- burger
- seafood
- grill
- casual-dining
- fine-dining

### Service Model

- full-service
- quick-service
- fast-casual
- takeaway-heavy
- delivery-heavy
- event-heavy

### Experience and Quality Positioning

- budget
- mid-range
- premium
- luxury
- quality-service
- high-end-food

### Operational Intensity

- high-volume
- high-precision
- high-turnover-workforce
- multi-location
- seasonal-demand-peak

## Combination Rule

A niche profile should combine 3-7 tags across these dimensions.

Example:

- `italian`
- `full-service`
- `premium`
- `quality-service`
- `high-end-food`
