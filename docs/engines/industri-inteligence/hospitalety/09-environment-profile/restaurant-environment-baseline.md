# Restaurant Environment Baseline

## Service Context

- Peak-heavy demand (lunch and dinner concentration)
- High interruption frequency during active service
- Fast decision windows for staffing and incident response

## Workforce Context

- Mixed digital maturity
- Mixed language proficiency
- High onboarding and turnover pressure

## Compliance Context

- Continuous food safety pressure
- Allergen communication is mission-critical
- Age control and responsible service checks for bar workflows

## Physical Context

- Multi-zone operations (kitchen, sal, bar, storage, hygiene zones)
- Frequent cross-zone handoffs
- Hygiene and contamination boundaries require explicit controls

## Technical Context

- Mobile-first for frontline actions
- Simple interactions under time pressure
- Auditable outputs for managers and compliance owners

## Practical Use

- Tune task pipeline strictness
- Tune journey complexity by role
- Tune testing scenarios for realistic pressure and edge cases
