# Business Structure Template Contract

## Purpose

Describe how an industry workspace should be structurally bootstrapped.

## Scope

- Departments and sub-domains
- Positions and role ladders
- Teams and leadership links
- Locations and zones
- Workforce cohorts
- Operating schedule assumptions

## Required Sections

1. Structural entities
2. Role-to-team mapping
3. Location/zone mapping
4. Baseline staffing mix
5. Seasonality assumptions
6. Compliance-critical role constraints

## Relevance

- Used during onboarding bootstrap and seed generation.
- Used by planning and scheduling modules for realistic defaults.
- Used by policy and journey templates as shared references.

## Restaurant Example Coverage

Restaurant template already provides this in SQL form via departments, locations, employees, teams, schedule, and contracts modules.
