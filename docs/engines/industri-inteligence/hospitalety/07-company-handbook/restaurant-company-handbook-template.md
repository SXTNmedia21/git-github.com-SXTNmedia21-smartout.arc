# Restaurant Company Handbook Template

## 1) Company Identity and Mission

- Mission statement
- Service promise
- Readiness definition for staff and shifts
- Brand tone and communication style

## 2) Organization Model

- Department list and purpose
- Team list and leadership assignment
- Role ladder and responsibilities
- Decision authority boundaries

## 3) Daily Operation Model

- Opening standard sequence
- Mid-service control sequence
- Closing and handover sequence
- Event day variant rules

## 4) Safety and Compliance

- Food safety requirements
- Hygiene and sanitation routines
- Allergen handling protocol
- Alcohol and age-control protocol
- Fire safety and evacuation protocol

## 5) Communication and Escalation

- Shift communication channels
- Incident reporting path
- Manager escalation matrix
- End-of-shift reporting format

## 6) Onboarding and Training

- Pre-boarding checklist
- Day 1 to Day 7 readiness milestones
- Mentor assignment model
- Knowledge test requirements

## 7) Shift and Scheduling Principles

- Shift planning principles
- Open-shift and replacement workflow
- Swap approval and compliance checks
- Overtime and underage restrictions

## 8) Quality and Service Standards

- Guest interaction standards
- Station readiness standards
- Service recovery standards
- Quality assurance checks

## 9) Incident Response

- Deviation categories
- Immediate containment actions
- Corrective action ownership
- Closure and prevention recording

## 10) KPI and Review Cadence

- Daily operational KPI review
- Weekly workforce and compliance review
- Monthly quality and retention review
- Action tracker and accountability owner
