# Restaurant Industry Research Pack

## 1) Industry Boundary

This package targets Norwegian hospitality operations with restaurant-heavy workflows and adjacent service modes (bar, catering, event, takeaway).

## 2) Workflow Definitions

### A. Staffing and Schedule Workflow

- Demand interpretation
- Shift construction
- Gap detection
- Open-shift publishing
- Swap/approval handling
- Cost and overtime verification
- Publish and acknowledgment

### B. Live Shift Operations Workflow

- Pre-open checklist
- Service readiness check
- Mid-shift controls
- Incident intervention
- Closing checklist
- Signoff and handover

### C. Food Safety and Compliance Workflow

- Varemottak and traceability check
- Temperature logs and variance control
- Hygiene and sanitation cycles
- Allergen control and communication
- Deviation logging and corrective action

### D. Onboarding and Readiness Workflow

- Pre-boarding and contract state
- Day-by-day onboarding tasks
- Course/test completion
- Mentor support loop
- Readiness promotion to active operator

### E. Multi-site Management Workflow

- Site-level KPI aggregation
- Site comparison and anomaly detection
- Corrective strategy rollout
- Local adaptation and re-measure

## 3) Workflow Summaries

- Managers optimize for speed, risk control, and labor-cost visibility.
- Frontline operators optimize for low-friction execution during service pressure.
- Back-office roles optimize for documentation integrity and audit readiness.
- New and language-constrained workers optimize for clarity and confidence.

## 4) Proven Knowledge

- Turnover is structurally high in this sector.
- High labor-cost pressure requires live staffing control.
- Digital maturity is uneven; one UX level cannot serve all worker groups.
- Compliance burden is real and operational, not administrative-only.
- Language and literacy barriers are central product constraints, not edge cases.
- The first 30 days of onboarding are retention critical.

## 5) Proven Tactics

- Mobile-first tasking for shift leaders and operators.
- Visual-first and low-text pathways for low-language profiles.
- Progressive disclosure: simple by default, depth on demand.
- Auto-suggestions for replacement staffing and policy-safe alternatives.
- Daily/weekly fixed check loops for safety-critical controls.
- Template-driven setup to reduce setup variance between workspaces.

## 6) Success Factors

- Time-to-action is low under real service pressure.
- Policy gates are embedded into workflow, not post-checked manually.
- Role-appropriate complexity per persona segment.
- Manager confidence in live state and predicted gaps.
- Clear handoff from onboarding to productive shift contribution.

## 7) KPI Framework

### Core Operational KPIs

- Time to fill open shifts
- Unfilled shift rate
- Overtime hours and overtime cost
- Labor cost percentage

### Workforce Readiness KPIs

- Time to job-ready
- Onboarding completion rate
- Protocol assignment completion rate
- Training test pass rate

### Safety and Compliance KPIs

- Temperature check completion rate
- Allergen process adherence rate
- Deviation frequency and closure time
- Hygiene checklist completion rate

### Product and Experience KPIs

- Task completion latency per persona segment
- Agent-correction frequency
- Abandonment rate by step
- Manual test failure clusters by journey step

## 8) Research-to-Template Relevance

- Workflows feed task pipeline templates.
- Workforce segmentation feeds AI council and test personas.
- Compliance insights feed default policy catalog.
- KPI framework feeds journey test assertions and event scoring.
