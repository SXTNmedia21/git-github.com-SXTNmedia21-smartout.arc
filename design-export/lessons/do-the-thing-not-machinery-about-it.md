# Decision lesson — do-the-thing-not-machinery-about-it

> Managed by sxtn-lesson-capture. The **Decision** block is canonical current truth.

---

## Decision

**Do the thing, not the machinery about the thing.** The default failure mode is to produce
meta-artifacts — specs, dashboards, watchdogs, worklists, lessons — while the founder sees no
product progress and the real goal is untouched. The bootstrap (get ONE page rendering real data)
is the actual value and must come FIRST; then build harness around proven motion. If a session has
hours of scaffolding and zero running product, the order is backwards. One running page > ten
planning docs.

**Founder pushback is a real signal — act on it fast, don't defend.** When the founder challenges
("are you on the right DB?", "we can just copy not rewrite", "you don't have a plan", "make it
small"), he is almost always seeing something true. Treat the challenge as data, reorient
immediately. Defending or re-explaining wastes his time and yours.

**Autonomy is a verb, not a claim.** Saying "self-perpetuating harness / I keep myself going" while
asking permission at every step is the opposite of autonomous. Autonomous = act → verify on disk →
report; surface only at genuine decision points (irreversible, or scoped to the human). Don't narrate
autonomy; perform it.

**Why:** Why: **Why:** A full session (2026-05-31) spent ~80% on scaffolding (specs, multiple
dashboards, watchdog crons, a foreman worklist) before getting the app to run and one page ported —
which was where all the real value landed, at the very end. Every time the founder pushed back he was
right (wrong DB container, copy-not-rewrite, no-real-plan). And despite bragging about a
self-perpetuating harness, the loop was "propose → ask → wait" the whole way. **How to apply:** open
every task by asking "what is the smallest real product result I can produce now?" and build it before
any machinery; when the founder challenges, reorient within the same turn; act-verify-report instead
of asking, reserving questions for irreversible or human-scoped decisions. Related: [[discovery-output-and-foreman-role]].
